Alaska Scale Service

Proudly serving Alaska for 20+ years in the sales, installation, calibration, and repair of industrial scales and weight systems.

Learn More

Based in Alaska's Mat-Su Valley

Alaska Scale Service was founded by Joe Roe in Alaska's beautiful Mat-Su Valley. Since opening, he has been traveling throughout the North Star state — from Unalaska Island to Kotzebue — installing, calibrating, repairing, and selling industrial scales for over 20 years. Our clients include Copper River Seafoods, Ocean Beauty Seafoods, Silver Bay Seafoods, and Trident Seafoods Inc.
